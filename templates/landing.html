About

Off-campus housing that actually feels built for you.

Perch is a housing platform designed specifically for students navigating the jump off campus. We partner with trusted local lessors and student communities to bring you listings that are transparent, vetted, and easy to compare.

From search to signing, we remove the guesswork: every listing includes the details students care about most — commute time to campus, amenities, real photos, and what it’s actually like to live there.

Whether you’re moving with friends or looking for a new roommate, Perch keeps everything in one place so you can focus on the next chapter, not the logistics.
